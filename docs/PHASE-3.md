# Phase 3 · A1 and Excel reconciliation

On the synthetic HTTPS demo, open **Data & reports**, replay September 2026 and open **DEMO-00001** from Casework. The case is in scope, its deadline is 16 October, and qualifying evidence is absent. Replay October 2026 and open the same case. The proof is dated 12 October and the separate official entry is absent. The result records on-time attainment and asks for reconciliation. The computed proof does not confer payment.

The first results detail sheet now exposes entry, scope, due status, recorded dates, undated official records and uncertain proof separately. The workbook includes control totals across people, scope, due-date completeness, all attainment, on-time attainment, late attainment, overdue cases and reviews. Both assignment and entry-year sheets use the same measures, including unknown buckets. The three language exports for each month were reopened with openpyxl. The detail sheet had 144 data rows and a filter covering every column. Both rollups reconciled to the control totals.

The [bundled A1 policy](../cockpit/a1-policy.json) defaults to German proof, age 16 on the report date and explicit status codes. An authorized local operator can supply another JSON file through the COCKPIT_A1_POLICY environment variable before launching the app. The policy hash is included in the rule version. The three-year deadline is a calendar anniversary, with 29 February clamped in a non-leap year. A deadline is due on the date and becomes overdue after that day. Future proof and future official entries are excluded. The public synthetic demo uses the bundled policy. ASD confirmation of qualifying language and proof, age reference, scope and official-record precedence remains open.
