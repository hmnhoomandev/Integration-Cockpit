# Photography

[Old town of Bern from the Rosengarten](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bern_Altstadt_von_Rosengarten_20211007.jpg), Daniel Kraft, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The bundled image is the 1,280-pixel Commons rendition. The app crops it to the display and adds a separate CSS scrim. The photograph retains its original licence.

## Civic marks

The Canton Bern flag and the Stadt Bern image were supplied with the design request. The Swiss flag is drawn in CSS. The Swiss Confederation shield and the SEM abbreviation refer to the [official SEM header](https://www.sem.admin.ch/de). The UI labels the marks as authorities referenced by an independent hackathon prototype.
