## Civic marks

The Canton Bern flag and the Stadt Bern image were supplied with the design request. `bern-flag.png` is a 256-pixel rendition of that flag; `stadt-bern-logo.png` and `stadt-bern-shield.png` are tight crops of the supplied Stadt Bern mark. The black-and-white Stadt Bern shield is the cockpit's own mark (sidebar and sign-in), because the tool is built for the City's asylum social service; the coloured canton flag appears with the other authorities. The Swiss flag is drawn in CSS. The interface palette (red, gold, bear black) is taken from these marks. The Swiss Confederation shield and the SEM abbreviation refer to the [official SEM header](https://www.sem.admin.ch/de). The UI labels the marks as authorities referenced by an independent hackathon prototype.
