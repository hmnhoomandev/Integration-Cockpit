# Phase 7 · Scenario, payments, reconciliation, workload and forecast

On the synthetic HTTPS demo, select **Demo account** or **Management** and replay October 2026 if necessary. Open **Forecast**. The 2027 comparison recalculates goals with the versioned candidate cutoff in memory and shows the difference per status. The stored snapshot, A1 deadline and exported responsibility dates are unchanged. When household cutoffs conflict in the scenario, the household stays in review. In this fixture, 48 person cutoffs change, A1 and employment attainment are stable, and 48 scoped households require review under the candidate. The cohort rule is illustrative.

In **Illustrative payments**, enter a separate CHF rate for each goal. The estimator includes only on-time computed results with matching official dates and reports uncorroborated and review cases. Household calculations use distinct Dossier-Nr. In the October fixture, the A1 estimate has 33 corroborated and six uncorroborated attainments. A hypothetical A1 rate of CHF 1,000 yields CHF 33,000. The report is not a legal or financial determination, and employment and household results lacking corroboration do not contribute.

In **Casework**, use the judge or authorized caseworker profile and open DEMO-00001. The ZEMIS section distinguishes missing overlapping employment from missing dates or unconfirmed employer alignment. The detail and Excel include source-row references. In **Overview → Caseload**, compare people and distinct households per assignment, 30-day follow-ups, cases needing review, and the unassigned bucket. A household can appear under multiple assignments, so the assignment rows are not an additive household total.

The **Forecast** panel shows the full month/count input series, one-month horizon, model-selection and validation sizes, validation-gate and held-out errors, and interval coverage. The 48-month synthetic default chooses last observation after the trend fails the earlier validation gate. Held-out MAE is 1.75 counts for the selected baseline and 3.36 for trend. The illustration estimates 43 for January 2026 with interval 33–53. The public FSO statistic is separate context. A local judge or manager can upload a consecutive aggregate CSV or reset to the synthetic series. Hosted data stays synthetic. Real forecasting and payment policy still need ASD-approved inputs and authority.
